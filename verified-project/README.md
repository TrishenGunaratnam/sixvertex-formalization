# Six vertex formalization

This project audits the original OpenAI six-vertex manuscript and begins its
Lean formalization. The Gaussian free field theorem remains unproved here.

The source is **The Gaussian free field limit of the balanced six-vertex model
with variance multiplier 1/arcsin(c/2)**, dated 23 September 2026 and released in
the October OAI mathematics dump. The downloaded PDF has **93 pages**, including
references.

## What is proved

**Lemma 2.1, “Finite row algebra” (`lem:row-algebra`), is fully formalized.**
The theorem `SixVertex.finite_row_algebra` in
[FiniteRowAlgebra.lean](lean/SixVertex/FiniteRowAlgebra.lean) proves all seven
clauses: the monodromy gauge, marked-row adjoint, commutation with a common
diagonal twist, full physical RTT, conjugation of joint auxiliary marks, and
the two homogeneous endpoint translations. It uses the paper's real
inhomogeneities, arbitrary complex spectral arguments and marks,
`0 < lambda < pi`, and even `L >= 4` from Section 1. Commutation includes
singular intertwiner differences, by a proved analytic-continuation argument.

There are **1457 checked Lean theorem declarations**. **Five of the paper's 51 named statements are proved in their stated setting, without external mathematical assumptions. Lemmas 2.4 and 2.5 and Corollary 2.6 are verified conditional on Slavnov's identity; 43 other named statements remain without complete formalizations.** The helper count is not a percentage of the paper.
Lemma 6.2, previously deferred, is now proved in [ChiralRepresentatives.lean](lean/SixVertex/ChiralRepresentatives.lean). It proves the standalone dense-core representative lemma, including extension, arbitrary strongly converging families, composition and the pairings-plus-norm criterion. The six-vertex-specific Gram convergence and tensor construction are not yet formalized.

**Lemma 7.6, logarithmic integrability (`lem:log-integrability`), is fully formalized** in [LogIntegrability.lean](lean/SixVertex/LogIntegrability.lean). It proves atomlessness and finite self and mixed absolute logarithmic energy from ordinary signed-measure Green integrability. The infinite diagonal, the exact Green normalization, measurable dyadic cells and all scale sums are checked. It also proves the equivalence with a null diagonal plus ordinary Bochner integrability, so the value of `Real.log 0` cannot weaken the premise. No external mathematical identity is used.

**Lemma 2.3, “Single-entry actions” (`lem:finite-actions`), is fully formalized** in
[SingleEntryActions.lean](lean/SixVertex/SingleEntryActions.lean). It proves the exact
B/A/L/C formulas for arbitrary off-shell spectral sets, including the ordered
two-removal C sum; explicit weighted expansions for every finite word; the bound
on changed roots; and the complete-expression limits at coincident arguments.
The latter use labelled lists and preserve multiplicities. The actual standard
monodromy and the vacuum products are identified with the paper's conventions,
and the ordinary vector action is also proved. The C proof uses an extra spin
site and does not divide by vacuum eigenvalues. No assumption from the deferred
lemmas, on-shell Bethe data, or scalar-product determinant is used.

**Lemma 2.2, “Finite Bethe data” (`lem:bethe-data`), is fully formalized** in
[FiniteBetheData.lean](lean/SixVertex/FiniteBetheData.lean). This includes the
counting-to-Bethe equations, both eigenvector statements with the physical
parity factor, the Gaudin Jacobian, the real analytic root branch and its
parameter derivative, positive definiteness for lambda >= pi/2, and the
weighted-matrix identity. The eigenvalue has a proved entire extension, so
spectral coincidences are included in the vector equations. The branch proof
uses Mathlib's proved analytic inverse-function theorem. This lemma uses no uniform inverse bound, scalar-product determinant or thermodynamic existence assumption.

**Lemma 2.4, “Exact scalar products and deleted columns,” is conditionally formalized** in
[ExactScalarProducts.lean](lean/SixVertex/ExactScalarProducts.lean). The sole external input is
`hSlavnov : SlavnovIdentity`, the generic derivative-over-Cauchy determinant formula.
The conversion to source columns, complex source-Gaudin limit (including the diagonal),
vacuum residues, exact Gaudin norm and nonvanishing, arbitrary deleted columns,
weighted minors and complete-expression coincident limits are proved in Lean.

**Lemma 2.5, “The Hilbert dual,” is conditionally formalized** in
[HilbertDual.lean](lean/SixVertex/HilbertDual.lean). The operator adjoint and physical
norm identity are proved without Slavnov. Nonzero norm, the unit physical vector,
and the overlap ratio retain A01 explicitly. The construction uses the actual L2
spin Hilbert space and includes arbitrary real staggered inhomogeneities.
Corollary 2.6 is in progress, with exact charge conservation and the initial path
quotient cancellations already proved.

### Assumed list

- **A01 — Twisted Slavnov identity** (`eq:Slavnov-exact`): assumed by explicit user instruction.
  [SlavnovAssumption.lean](lean/SixVertex/SlavnovAssumption.lean) defines the exact proposition;
  every conditional result retains it as an explicit hypothesis. No global Lean axiom was added.
  [Machine-readable assumed list](progress/assumptions.json).

The three earlier named lemmas do not depend on A01. Lemmas 2.4 and 2.5 and Corollary 2.6 remain conditional until
that identity is proved. None of the infinite-volume estimates is assumed or marked complete.

The starter currently proves finite combinatorial results: the six allowed
vertices; ice-rule conservation; arrow-reversal symmetries; periodic balanced
sector conservation; local consistency and uniqueness of four face heights;
soundness, completeness and absence of duplicates in the glued-column
enumerator; transfer-kernel sector support and degree bounds; and c-free balanced
witnesses at every even height. It also constructs exact positive-rational
single-column Boltzmann weights and proves normalization of their probability law.

The transfer kernel is represented by monomial exponents, with an exponent `k`
standing for `c^k`. Its rational evaluation is proved equal to the sum over the
duplicate-free column configurations. For positive rational `c`, its mass is
positive exactly when an admissible column exists; incompatible arrow-count
sectors have zero mass. Column probabilities are nonnegative, positive on the
support and sum to one when the support is nonempty.

The row-algebra module uses the actual complex matrices. The separate
single-column probability module still treats rational Boltzmann weights.
Real-valued finite probability measures, the multi-column torus law, its
partition-function trace identity and all infinite-volume convergence results
remain open.

The source correspondence, parameter assumptions and representation conventions
are recorded in [the named-statement record](progress/named-statements.json).
The proof files are `RowAlgebra`, `RowGauge`, `YangBaxter`, `RowTrain`,
`FiniteRTT`, `RowCommutation`, `RowEndpoints` and the assembled `FiniteRowAlgebra`.
No mathematical mistake was found in any of the three completed named lemmas.

## Recheck

Requires Lean 4.35.0, Lake, and Python 3. The row-algebra proofs add Mathlib
v4.35.0, pinned by `lean/lake-manifest.json` to
`56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679`. The Lean toolchain was not changed.
The dependencies and the required precompiled library modules are installed
under `lean/.lake/packages`.

```sh
python3 scripts/check.py
python3 scripts/render_progress.py
```

The check builds the library, inspects the axioms of every declared theorem, and
recomputes all downloaded source hashes. The allowed foundational axioms are
`propext`, `Quot.sound`, and `Classical.choice`; `sorryAx` is not permitted.
No axiom standing for an unproved mathematical estimate is added.
